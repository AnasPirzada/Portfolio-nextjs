# PRODUCT.md — Anas Pirzada Portfolio

**Register:** brand (design IS the product — a portfolio whose job is to win clients).

## What this is
A personal portfolio for **Anas Pirzada**, a Full Stack Developer & AI Expert (5+ years). The
site's commercial job is **client acquisition**: a visitor (a founder, agency, or hiring manager)
should land, trust the craft within seconds, and book a call ("Let's Talk" → Calendly) or reach out.
Fiverr / Upwork badges reinforce credibility.

## Audience
Prospective clients and collaborators evaluating whether Anas can ship polished, modern, AI-capable
web products. They judge the developer by the portfolio itself — so the site's own craft is the
single strongest proof of skill. "AI-generated-looking" output actively hurts the pitch.

## Voice (three physical-object words)
**Warm · precise · quietly confident.** A well-made object — think a brass-and-cream editorial press
kit, not a neon dev-tool dashboard. Refined, not loud; expensive-feeling through restraint and
craft, not through effects.

## Brand identity (already committed — preserve, don't reinvent)
- **Palette:** Warm Pearl light mode (`#f7f4ef` bg, `#1a1410` ink, `#c8860a` gold accent) and a
  black/gold dark mode (`#000` bg, `#fff` ink, `#efc041` gold). Gold is the throughline.
- **Theme:** warm editorial. The brief is to **elevate** this to high-end, not replace it.
  (Note: warm-cream editorial is a saturated AI lane — so distinctiveness must come from craft,
  motion, and typographic rigor, NOT from leaning harder on cream + serif + tracked eyebrows.)

## Homepage narrative (section order)
Hero → About → Skills → Projects → Tagline → Education/Certifications → Work/Experience →
Blogs → Resume → Collaboration → Contact → Footer.

## Constraints
- Stack: Next.js 14 (pages router), Tailwind, Framer Motion (`m` via LazyMotion), GSAP +
  ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll, SCSS modules. Dark + light themes. i18n / RTL present.
- Must stay fully responsive (phone → ultrawide) and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep existing features working (voice assistant, Calendly, badges, blog/projects routes).
